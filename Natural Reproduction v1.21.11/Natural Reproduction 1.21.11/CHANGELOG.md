# Changelog - Natural Reproduction (MC 1.21.11)

All notable changes to **Natural Reproduction** for Minecraft 1.21.11 are documented in this file.

## [1.4.31+1.21.11] - 2026-09-27

### Added & Multi-Era Compatibility
- **Minecraft 1.21.11 Winter Drop Port**:
  - Full compatibility and dedicated standalone JAR for Minecraft 1.21.11.
  - Native support for 1.21.11 animal entity traits, wolf variants, and environment triggers.
  - Java 21 bytecode and Loom 1.15-SNAPSHOT remap tooling.
- **DasikLibrary 1.1.0+1.21.11 Integration**:
  - Powered by the dedicated DasikLibrary 1.1.0+1.21.11 runtime for autonomous breeding, genetics, and dynamic GameRules.
- **Autonomous Reproduction Core Mechanics**:
  - Autonomous breeding across passive livestock with density cap gating.
  - Multi-generational inbreeding degradation and hybrid vigor outcrossing.
  - Pasture enrichment, feeding troughs, and rotational grazing wear.
  - Pregnancy gestation timers and prenatal pasture nourishment.
  - Herd social cohesion, alpha leadership election, and coordinated stampede flight.
  - Zero-allocation spatial partitioning and staggered tick modulo throttling.
