Natural Reproduction

Delayed Gratification Collection | Pasture Management & Multi-Generational Investment

Animals in the wild should not depend solely on players for survival and growth. Natural Reproduction allows all passive mobs in Minecraft to autonomously breed and multiply when environmental conditions are ideal, while rewarding thoughtful pasture management, native climate alignment, and multi-generational size genetics investment.

🌟 Key Features

  • 🌾 Autonomous Wild Breeding: Healthy, well-fed animals naturally enter Love Mode when surrounded by compatible mates and environmental habitat blocks.
  • 🏞️ Native Biome Climate Fertility Boost: Animals in their native climate biomes (e.g. Wolves in Taigas, Frogs in Swamps, Cows/Sheep in Plains, Polar Bears in Ice Plains) receive 2x faster breeding frequency and +15% offspring genetics quality boost.
  • 🎨 Biome Variant Skin Adaptation: Offspring born in specific biomes dynamically adapt visual skin variants (e.g. Desert Frogs become Warm variant, Taiga Wolves become Pale/Black variant, Snowy Rabbits become White variant).
  • 🐺 Variant-Specific Environmental Habitat Triggers: Animals require variant-tailored habitat blocks (e.g. Snowy Wolves & Foxes require snow/ice blocks; Warm Frogs & Gold Rabbits require sand/cactus).
  • 🏚️ Cramped Space Penalty vs. 🌾 Spacious Pasture Recovery: Breeding in cramped 4x4 factory farm pens (4+ mobs) stunts offspring scale down to 0.25x. Moving animals to spacious open pastures (+30% recovery per generation) restores physical size potential up to 1.30x!
  • ⚖️ Scale-Based Item Drops: Item drop yield on death/harvest scales proportionally with physical body scale attribute.
  • ⚙️ 27 Per-Species GameRule Toggles: Full granular control over every single animal species under a dedicated Natural Reproduction - Species Toggles category (all enabled by default).

📋 Dynamic GameRules

  • natural-reproduction:enabled (Boolean, Default: true) — Master switch for autonomous wild breeding.
  • natural-reproduction:density_cap (Integer, Default: 10) — Maximum same-species animals permitted within a 16-block radius.
  • natural-reproduction:rate (Integer, Default: 24000) — Average tick interval between breeding checks (24000 = 1 MC Day).
  • natural-reproduction:scale_drops (Boolean, Default: true) — Scale-proportional item drop yields.
  • natural-reproduction:cramped_space_penalty (Boolean, Default: true) — Confined pen stunting vs spacious pasture size recovery.
  • natural-reproduction:biome_fertility (Boolean, Default: true) — 2x breeding speed and +15% quality in native biomes.
  • natural-reproduction:biome_variants (Boolean, Default: true) — Dynamic biome variant skin adaptation for offspring.
  • natural-reproduction:allow_<species> (Boolean, Default: true) — 27 individual species toggles under Natural Reproduction - Species Toggles category (Cows, Pigs, Sheep, Chickens, Horses, Wolves, Frogs, Bees, Hoglins, etc.).

💬 Brigadier Command Suite

Manage all mod settings in-game via /naturalreproduction with tab completion:

  • /naturalreproduction status — Display active states of all core rules and 27 species toggles.
  • /naturalreproduction get <rule> — Query the current value of a specific rule.
  • /naturalreproduction set <rule> <value> — Dynamically update any core rule or species toggle.
  • /naturalreproduction trackerlogs list|clear|enable|disable — Inspect and manage autonomous breeding event logs.
  • /naturalreproduction reset — Reset all rules and 27 species toggles to defaults.
