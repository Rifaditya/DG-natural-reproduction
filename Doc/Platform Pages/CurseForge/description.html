🌿 Natural Reproduction

"Ecology in Motion. Autonomous Breeding, Herd Dynamics, and Pasture Ecosystems."

📖 Introduction

In vanilla Minecraft, animal life is strangely artificial and static. Cows, sheep, pigs, and chickens stand frozen in cramped pens, completely incapable of breeding, thriving, or expanding unless a player manually walks up and hand-feeds them. The moment you leave your farm, the ecosystem grinds to a dead halt. Wild animal populations never grow, natural herd structures do not exist, and farming devolves into industrial 1x1 breeding pits.

Natural Reproduction breathes vibrant, organic life into Minecraft's wildlife under the Delayed Gratification design philosophy. Animals become dynamic ecological beings — they form cohesive social herds, follow established herd leaders, and reproduce autonomously when their environment satisfies natural biological requirements. Built with strict spatial caching algorithms and high-performance tick budgeting, ecosystems flourish without lagging your world.

✨ Features

Autonomous Animal Reproduction

  • 27 animal species breed autonomously when environmental conditions are met. No player hand-feeding required.
  • Per-species GameRule toggles allow enabling or disabling autonomous reproduction for each species independently.

Data-Driven Animal Genetics

  • Inherited Trait System: Offspring inherit genetics across three traits — scale (body size), max_health (HP), and movement_speed — from parents via the DasikLibrary Genetics API.
  • Scale-Based Item Drops: Animals with higher scale attributes drop proportionally more items when harvested (natural-reproduction:scale_drops).
  • Cramped Space Penalty: Breeding in inhumane feedlots stunts offspring size down to 0.10x; breeding in spacious pastures recovers healthy genetics (natural-reproduction:cramped_space_penalty).
  • Inbreeding Lineage Degradation: Closed-herd inbreeding degrades genetics over generations (natural-reproduction:inbreeding_degradation).
  • Size Bounds: Configurable minimum (default 10%), normal (default 95%), and maximum (default 120%) size percentages via GameRules.

Environmental Fertility and Gestation Cycles

  • Population Density Cap: Autonomous mating halts once a species reaches its carrying capacity within 16 blocks (default: 10 animals, natural-reproduction:density_cap).
  • Autonomous Gestation Timers: Expectant mothers enter a pregnancy countdown before delivering offspring (default: 24,000 ticks = 1 MC day, natural-reproduction:gestation_period + natural-reproduction:gestation_duration).
  • Manual Breeding Gestation: Player hand-feeding also triggers the gestation cycle rather than instant birth (natural-reproduction:manual_gestation).
  • Pasture Enrichment: Pastures with feeding troughs, hay, water, and shelter grant well-nourished bonuses to breeding frequency (natural-reproduction:pasture_enrichment).
  • Overgrazing Terrain Wear: Dense herds convert grass blocks into dirt and coarse dirt over time (natural-reproduction:overgrazing).

Chicken Egg System

  • Fertilized Chicken Eggs: Autonomous chicken breeding rolls 50/50 for immediate chicks or a guaranteed-hatch Fertilized Egg (natural-reproduction:fertilized_chicken_eggs).
  • Infertile Regular Eggs: Ordinary unfertilized eggs have a reduced 1/64 miracle hatch chance (natural-reproduction:chicken_infertile_regular_eggs).
  • Dispenser Egg Hatch Chance: Fertilized Eggs fired from dispensers have a configurable hatch chance (default 75%, natural-reproduction:dispenser_egg_hatch_chance).

Herd Dynamics and Social Behavior

  • Social Herd Clustering: Animals dynamically organize into herds led by the largest Alpha animal with diurnal cohesion schedules (natural-reproduction:herd_dynamics).
  • Herd Stampede Panic: Damage from predators or players alerts nearby herd members to stampede away in unison (natural-reproduction:herd_stampede).

Biome Ecology

  • Native Biome Fertility Boost: Animals in native biomes breed 2x faster with +15% offspring genetics quality (natural-reproduction:biome_fertility).
  • Biome Variant Skin Adaptation: Offspring born in specific biomes adapt their visual entity variant skin dynamically (natural-reproduction:biome_variants).
